Pocket / Alba: optional, separately installed speech engine

No Pocket native runtime, model weights or voice recording is bundled in OpenAuto AI.
This app can explicitly connect to the evaluated local Android engine. The provider
response text is sent locally to that selected engine; no key or chat archive is sent.

Pocket TTS Android Engine contributors, 2026; MIT License.
Source: https://github.com/The-unknown-Shadowman/PocketTTS-Android-Engine
Evaluated baseline: 37809d23d68ea17fdeb5faad5edc97771022cf9a (0.5.3).
Our cancellation patch keeps synthesis synchronous and guards request/native lifetime.
